Oh C'mon Dude !

BLOGS by ARCHITA JAISWAL — SEP 19, 2026

A few days ago, I met one of my female friends after a long time.

Not that long, but yeah, long enough for us to have a LOT to catch up on.

It wasn't like we hadn't interacted at all during these months. We just hadn't really sat down and had a proper talk-talk in a long time. So she told me how her life had been lately, what had happened over the past two or three months, and obviously, I did the same.

We talked for hours that day.

But that's not actually the point.

The actual thing is that after all those stories and lore, we somehow ended up hitting a lot of small-small realisations and little reality checks.

I don't even know where to start, so I'm just going to write whatever comes to my mind first.

First of all, there are people in college who will form negative opinions about you and then try to portray you as a bad person in front of others, behind your back.

And sometimes they'll do this within the first few days or months of knowing you.

Like... why?

And I genuinely don't understand why some people only seem to have NEGATIVE opinions—and that too, about EVERYONE.

Like, c'mon dude.

Do you ever have anything nice to say?

Then there are people who absolutely LOVE commenting on or judging women based on their appearance, choice of clothing, skin colour, face structure, body, or basically anything remotely physical.

PLEASE. HAVE SOME SHAME.

Because honestly, it reflects way more about your upbringing and your personality than it ever will about the woman you're judging.

And it doesn't stop there.

They crack misogynistic and racist jokes too, and somehow think it's cool.

"Wow! I'm a racist."

"Wow! I'm a misogynist."

And then they call it dark humour.

NO.

DUMBASS.

IT'S NOT.

Don't even get me started on rejections.

If you reject someone or simply tell them "no," suddenly they'll start saying shitty things about you, your face, your body, your personality—basically anything they can find—to everyone except you.

And the moment you turn towards them?

Suddenly they're all cool.

Nothing happened.

Everything's fine.

Because guess what?

Their ego is apparently as fragile as their balls, and they don't have the guts to say any of that shit to your face.

AND OMG.

This last one is genuinely crazy to me.

Men in college being completely okay with dating women who were literally their second choice.

My God.

What about your girl buddy?

How is she supposed to feel when she eventually finds out that you asked her out ONLY BECAUSE the person you actually wanted rejected you?

And then there's another thing I've noticed.

Some guys are perfectly okay dating someone who is completely opposite to their supposed "type."

And I'm not just making this up, okay?

Because every time I ask a guy what his type is, he starts listing attributes that are basically the complete opposite of his girlfriend.

So my question is—

Why did you even commit to her then?

Like, what was the thought process here?

So yeah...

People are very weird in college.

Especially men.

But honestly, that's okay.

Because somewhere between all these weird interactions, unnecessary drama, bad experiences, awkward situations, and little reality checks, you also get a little bit of character development.

You learn to take a stand for yourself.

You learn what kind of people you want around you.

You learn what you absolutely don't want.

And most importantly, you slowly start becoming more prepared for the weirdness that comes with adult life.

But don't worry.

There are good people too.

You just have to keep your hopes high, take your time, and let yourself build new connections.

Not everyone is weird.

You just have to survive enough weird people to find the good ones.
