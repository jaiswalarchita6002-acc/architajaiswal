A Chapter

BLOGS by ARCHITA JAISWAL — 24 OCT, 2026

It all started the day I joined the town's renowned JEE coaching institute. It was my first lecture there. I still remember how much of a social awkwardieee I was back then, and what a super anxious person I was to be inside that big cuboid where they conducted the subject sessions.

So, just like any other anxious kid, I did what they would do — find somebody who's quiet and seems non-judgy, ofc.

SO, from a distance, I spotted this one girl. She was wearing a full-sleeve denim jacket in freaking peak summer, btw, so I quickly understood that she was probably insecure about some part of her arms or smth like that. Because not only was she wearing that full-sleeve jacket, but she was constantly dragging the sleeves down towards her palms, all the way to her knuckles. And on top of that, she was repeatedly tucking her short bangs behind her ears (btw, she had a side hair parting).

And then I walked up to her and said, "Hey, can I sit with u?"

She said nothing and simply nodded her head in agreement.

I quickly took the seat beside her and attended all the lectures sitting next to her. The last lecture was about to finish, and that's when I finally decided to ask her. So I said, "Ughhh! Hey, would u mind if we attend the lectures daily TOGETHER?"

To which she replied, "Okay."

Yeah, that was all.

And since that day, all the way till the end of 11th grade, we've been attending all the lectures together.

And u know what happens when u choose to sit with the same person through all four seasons? YESS, u get to know each other's past stories, family backgrounds, insecurities, strengths, talents, etc., etc. U create this bond which is, in one word — AMAZING!

11th grade got over, and I, for some reason which idk till date, decided to leave that institute and switch to online coaching. Which also meant that we both got separated from there. Since our homes were also quite far apart and she didn't know how to ride a scooty, naturally, I would sometimes visit her place — all the way from my home to hers, about 18 kms.

Ikr, FREAKING 18 KMS.

But u know, friendship has no limits. And just like that, we used to update each other about our lives through calls and msgs, and I would go meet her sometimes. I even used to post her birthday stories, wish her exactly at 12 midnight — all these years I've known her.

And just like I didn't realise it, time flew by, and her day of JEE Advanced came.

She gave the exam. I won't talk much about her rank or marks, but she got admission into one of the top IITs.

I was happy for her. LITERALLY. So freaking happy.

We were so excited. We literally used to discuss the curtain colours, aesthetic dorm-room aesthetics, and all that stuff. Even after joining college, we were still connected through calls and msgs.

But after some time, I noticed that she started texting less and less, and our calls became quite rare. And slowly, we stopped talking completely.

And it became this weird thing where she would see my stories and posts, I would see hers, and I would like hers — but that was the only difference.

No texts. No calls. Just simply existing behind screens.

Idk why, but I used to think of her as my best friend, because there was literally nothing left between the two of us that we hadn't shared with each other.

I knew for a fact that she already had a best friend even before I met her, but if not a best friend, I thought she at least used to think of me as a really close friend. But anyways, I felt extremely bad, and I didn't know what to do with that feeling. So, I simply tried to keep my distance and give her her space, and thought of it like this:

"If I mean enough to her as a really good friend, she'll text me eventually. But if she doesn't, it's fine. Not everyone stays. Some people are like chapters in your story, not the whole plot, obv. So I'll just let her go, simply."

Hmm. This had been on my mind for quite a long time, so I thought of sharing it with u guys.

Anyways, what I have understood from all this is that DISTANCE DOES HAVE A MAJOR ROLE IN FRIENDSHIPS, RELATIONSHIPS, LITERALLY ANY KIND OF BOND YOU MAKE WITH PEOPLE.

And that ONE-SIDED EFFORT MEANS NOTHING AND HAS NO EFFECT IF THE OTHER PERSON DOESN'T VALUE THE BOND THE SAME WAY U DO.
